## Upper bound (explicit construction)

Eight-input CCZ cube factory followed by a Y measurement of one output qubit (Proposition 10: projecting the third
qubit of |CCZ> onto (|0> +- i|1>)/sqrt2 leaves CS^{+-1}|++>). Order two is inherited from the cube factory.

- [`scripts/control_cube.py`](../../scripts/control_cube.py)
- [`scripts/worked_example.py`](../../scripts/worked_example.py)

```bash
python control_cube.py
```
