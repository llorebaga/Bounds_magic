## Lower bound

Lemma 13 (trading order for inputs): for non-stabilizer tau, n_min(tau, d) >= n_min(tau, s) + d - s, by feeding d - s inputs with maximally mixed qubits I/2 = rho_T(1/2). With n_min(T, 2) >= 9 ([d2](d2.md)) this gives n >= 11; the analytic bound 2d = 8 is weaker here.
